# First-10 Prospect List — Criteria & Capture Sheet

_Created 2026-07-21 per board verdict: grow the list from 3 → 10, send
messages 4–7 by Friday 2026-07-24. Replies interrupt everything._

## Who qualifies (all four must be true)

1. **Sends proposals or quotes to win work.** Not salaried, not
   marketplace-only (Upwork/Fiverr escrow replaces the proposal→invoice
   chain, so marketplace-only freelancers feel no pain).
2. **Invoices clients directly** — Stripe, PayPal, bank transfer, or worse
   (Word doc + prayer). The "or worse" people are the best prospects.
3. **Small enough that paperwork is personal.** Solo up to ~3 people. Once
   there's an admin/bookkeeper, the pain moved to someone you can't reach.
4. **Reachable warm or lukewarm.** You know them, know someone who knows
   them, or share a community where you already participate. Cold DMs to
   strangers are the post's job, not the list's.

## Strong-fit niches (where deals die in the paperwork gaps)

- Videographers / photographers (per-project, deposits, usage rights)
- Web/app freelance devs and designers
- Consultants & coaches (B2B proposals — like the sister's AI-training startup)
- Trades with quotes: handymen, detailers, cleaners with commercial clients
- Event services: DJs, planners, caterers
- Marketing/social-media freelancers

## Disqualifiers

- Marketplace-only income (no direct client paperwork)
- Already runs HoneyBook/Dubsado/Bonsai *and likes it* (switching cost >
  early-product value; note the tool they use — that's competitor intel)
- Retainer-only with one long-term client (sends ~1 proposal a year)

## Where to mine names (aim: 10 in one downtime session)

Phone contacts → Instagram follows (local service businesses you already
follow) → Mars's network (he knows operators) → sister's startup circle →
former coworkers who freelance on the side → the filmmaker friend's crew
(one warm intro each is cheaper than one cold post).
