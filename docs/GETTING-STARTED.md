# Sealed — Getting Started

Sealed takes a client from **proposal → signed agreement → paid invoice** in one flow, one link. No juggling a doc app, a signature app, and an invoice app.

**App:** https://sealed.techtrendwire.com

---

## 1. Create your account (2 minutes)

1. Go to https://sealed.techtrendwire.com and click **Sign up**.
2. Sign up with your email (or Google). That's it — your workspace is created automatically on first sign-in.
3. You land on the **Dashboard**: stats across your proposals, agreements, and invoices, plus recent activity.

> On a phone, tap the ☰ menu (top left) to open navigation.

## 2. Add your client (1 minute)

1. Go to **Contacts** → **Add Contact**.
2. Enter their name and email (company optional). The email matters — it's where their proposal will be sent.

## 3. Create and send a proposal

1. Go to **Proposals** → **Create Proposal**.
2. Pick the contact, give it a clear title (e.g. *"Two-Day Corporate AI Training — Acme Co"*).
3. Add **line items**: description, quantity, unit price. Add tax if applicable. Totals calculate automatically.
4. Optionally set an expiration date (creates natural urgency).
5. Save, review, then hit **Send**.

Your client gets an email with a private link. They see a clean, professional proposal page — no account or login needed on their end — and can **Accept** (typing their name as consent) or **Decline**. You see the status change on your dashboard: SENT → VIEWED → ACCEPTED.

> **⚠️ Check spam the first time.** Emails come from `sealed@techtrendwire.com`, a new sending domain, so Gmail often files the *first* message in **Spam**. Tell your client to check their spam folder and mark it **"Not spam"** — after that, future emails land in the inbox. (Same applies to the signature and invoice emails in the next steps.)

## 4. Turn the accepted proposal into a signed agreement

1. Once the proposal shows **ACCEPTED**, go to **Agreements** → create one from that proposal.
2. Hit **Send for Signature**. The client gets the agreement to e-sign (powered by DocuSeal — legally binding).
3. When they sign, the agreement flips to **SIGNED** automatically. No chasing, no "did you get my email?"

## 5. Invoice and get paid

1. Once the agreement is **SIGNED**, go to **Invoices** → create one from that agreement.
2. Hit **Send**. The client gets an invoice email with a secure **Pay** link (powered by Stripe — they pay by card).
3. When they pay, the invoice flips to **PAID** automatically and the payment is recorded.

Done. Quote to cash, one thread, full status visibility the whole way.

---

## The rules of the flow (why buttons are sometimes missing)

Sealed enforces the order on purpose — it's the whole point:

- An **agreement** can only be created from an **ACCEPTED** proposal.
- An **invoice** can only be created from a **SIGNED** agreement.

If you don't see a "create agreement/invoice" option, the previous step isn't complete yet.

## Tips

- **Emails come from** `sealed@techtrendwire.com` — first-time recipients (you *and* your clients) should check spam and mark **"Not spam"** once; inbox delivery follows from there.
- **Statuses are live.** SENT / VIEWED / ACCEPTED / SIGNED / PAID update from real events (opens, signatures, payments) — the dashboard is the truth.
- **Works on your phone.** Full dashboard is mobile-friendly; check a proposal status from anywhere.
